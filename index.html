About A Round Tuit

You've been meaning to get a round tuit. Well, here's a whole deck of them. Each task, chore, or errand becomes a circular card — a "tuit" — that lives in one or more stacks.

STACKS & TUITS

Stacks are your categories — Chores, Groceries, Shopping, Projects, and Fitness & Health come built in, and you can add, rename, recolour, or delete any stack (tap its title while viewing it). A single tuit can belong to several stacks at once — check as many as you like when adding or editing one.

THE SHUFFLE

A stack's order stays put between visits — nothing reshuffles on its own. "Next Round Tuit" pages through the stack as it currently stands, without changing anything. "Shuffle Stack" redraws the whole stack's order at once, weighted by each tuit's priority: higher priority tends to land nearer the top, lower priority can land anywhere — but nothing is ever pinned in place, even after a shuffle. "Done" clears a one-and-done tuit for good, or cycles a repeating one back into the deck at a fresh, priority-weighted spot.

FLIP FOR SETTINGS

Tap a card to flip it. The back holds everything: edit the text, adjust priority, toggle "keeps coming back," change which stacks it belongs to, set a due date and reminders, or delete it.

TIME-SENSITIVE

Give any tuit a due date and it automatically appears in the Time-Sensitive stack too — no manual filing needed. That stack is sorted soonest-to-latest instead of shuffled, and ‹ › arrows let you browse through it in order.

REMINDERS

Any due tuit can carry one or more reminders (e.g. "1 day before" and "2 hours before"). With spoken reminders on (Settings), the app announces them out loud — but only while this tab is open. For reminders that work even when the app is closed, use "📅 Calendar" to export — your reminder times travel with it as real alarms in your phone's calendar app.

DONE PILE

Each stack's "Done" pill shows everything you've finished there. Restore anything back into its stack, or empty the whole pile.

EXPORT

The 📤 button turns any set of stacks into a downloadable .txt file, organized under headings with due dates included.

YOUR DATA

Everything is stored locally in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there's no account — which also means clearing your browser data or switching devices will clear your tuits, so export to text or calendar if you want a backup.
